About the Program

Unlocking venture capital for South East Nigeria.

The South East Venture Capital Program is an initiative of the South East Development Commission, designed to unlock venture capital, support high-potential founders, and catalyse long-term economic growth across the region.

Our mandate

A $50 million blended finance fund, built for a region the market has overlooked.

Through the creation of a $50 million blended finance Venture Capital Fund, the program addresses the capital and ecosystem gaps facing startups in the region — while strengthening the broader digital, innovation and technology ecosystem that startups depend on.

Core objectives

Four strategic priorities.

Each priority is designed to accelerate the growth of the regional digital, innovation and technology economy.

01

Close early-stage funding gaps

Provide capital for promising startups that struggle to access institutional investment at the earliest stages.

02

De-risk capital for private investors

Use blended finance structures that encourage venture funds, angel investors and institutions to back startups addressing developmental challenges.

03

Strengthen the regional startup ecosystem

Support founders, accelerators, innovation hubs and ecosystem partners building the infrastructure for entrepreneurship.

04

Promote inclusive entrepreneurship

Expand access to opportunity for founders across sectors, industries and communities throughout the South East.

How the program works

Five integrated workstreams.

SEVCP operates as one program with five connected parts — capital, discovery, company building and partnerships working together rather than in isolation.

01

Venture Capital Fund

A $50M blended finance vehicle combining public, DFI, institutional, private and diaspora capital — tickets from $50,000 to $2 million per company.

02

Pitch Competition

The primary investment pipeline for the Fund, run as a multi-phase national competition across the five states and the diaspora.

03

Incubation & Acceleration

Structured post-award support for founders across two tracks — Incubation for earlier-stage founders, Accelerator for growth-stage ventures.

04

Financing Partnerships

Co-investment alongside DFIs, institutional investors, family offices and diaspora capital.

05

Implementing Partnerships

Delivery, Resource and Network partners — hubs, universities, corporates and industry associations.

Where we invest

Technology-enabled ventures from or for the South East.

The Fund invests at pre-seed, seed and early growth stages (up to Series A) in ventures that originate from — or primarily impact — South East Nigeria.

Who we work with

Four ways to partner.

The program is built with public institutions, capital providers and the organisations that make up the regional ecosystem.

Financing Partners

DFIs, institutional investors, private capital and diaspora investors participating in the Fund.

Delivery Partners

Hubs, accelerators and operators running programme delivery on the ground across the five states.

Resource Partners

Corporates, universities and professional firms contributing expertise, facilities and market access.

Network Partners

Industry associations and ecosystem organisations extending reach to founders and customers.

Invest in the South East's future.

Whether you bring capital, expertise or reach, there is a role for you in accelerating innovation and entrepreneurship across the region.

Program

The SEVCP Pitch Competition.

A national competition that identifies high-potential startups from across the South East and connects them with capital, mentorship and structured ecosystem support. It is the primary pipeline into the South East Venture Capital Fund.

Two tracks

Founders apply to the track that matches their stage.

Every edition runs two tracks. Both lead to equity investment and a 12–24 week program.

Track 01

Accelerator Track

Traction → Scale

For startups with a live product and measurable early traction that are ready to scale.

Typical characteristics
—
A live product already in the market
—
Clear customer validation and early traction: paying customers, recurring usage, signed pilots or revenue growth
—
Typically 12–24 months of operating history, or equivalent evidence of sustained execution
—
A strong founding team with relevant domain and execution experience
—
An operational base and active market presence in South East Nigeria
From $20,000
Minimum equity investment, tranched
12–24 wks
Accelerator program
Track 02

Incubation Track

Idea → Validation

For earlier-stage startups still validating their product, customer segment and business model.

Typical characteristics
—
A working prototype or validated MVP
—
Early evidence of market interest: user testing, pilots, waitlists, partnerships or early adoption
—
A committed founding team able to participate fully in the program
—
Up to 12 months of operational activity, or earlier-stage evidence of consistent venture development
—
An operational base and active market presence in South East Nigeria
From $5,000
Minimum equity investment, tranched
12–24 wks
Incubation program
Selection process

Four phases, then finals.

Every edition follows the same structure. Exact dates are published when applications open.

01

Applications

Startups submit an online application: pitch deck, founder information, product description, traction or MVP evidence and funding request.

02

Screening & scoring

Applications are evaluated against a structured scoring system covering problem relevance, solution viability, execution, market opportunity and technology integration.

03

Video pitch

Shortlisted startups submit a three-minute video pitch covering their solution, traction or MVP validation, founding team and funding request.

04

Virtual pitch

Selected startups deliver a seven-minute pitch to a judging panel of venture investors, industry experts and ecosystem leaders.

05

Finalist bootcamp

Finalists join a one-week intensive bootcamp in Enugu to prepare for the final pitch.

06

Finals Day & Investment Ceremony

Finalists pitch live in Enugu, the judging panel selects the winners, and equity investment is awarded at the Investment Ceremony the following day.

What winners receive

Equity investment, a program, and a network.

Milestone-based investment

A minimum of $20,000 for Accelerator winners and $5,000 for Incubation winners — final amounts determined by the SEVCP Investment Committee and disbursed in milestone-based tranches.

A 12–24 week program

Accelerator companies focus on scaling and investor readiness; Incubation companies on product development and business validation. Length is set by what the company needs, within 12–24 weeks.

Mentorship

Guidance from experienced founders, investors and industry experts through the length of the program.

Investor exposure

Interactions with venture capital firms and angel investors, plus preparation for future venture funding.

Ecosystem access

Connections to regional innovation hubs, industry partners, startup networks and future funding opportunities.

Questions

Before you apply.

Who can apply?

Startups operating in or connected to the South East with a technology-enabled business model.

Do startups need revenue to apply?

No. Early-stage startups with a validated MVP can apply through the Incubation Track.

How many startups win?

There is no fixed number. The size of each cohort is set by the SEVCP Investment Committee on the recommendation of the judging panel.

Is the investment equity?

Yes. Winners receive equity investment disbursed in milestone-based tranches, alongside a place in a structured program.

Where do the finals take place?

Finals Day and the Investment Ceremony are held in Enugu, Nigeria.

When does the next edition open?

Dates for each edition are published here and in Program Updates. Join the pipeline to be notified first.
